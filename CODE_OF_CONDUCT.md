# Code of conduct

Hoolicy contributors must keep collaboration respectful, technical, and safe.

Welcome people regardless of background or experience. Critique ideas and evidence, not people. Do not harass, threaten, demean, expose private information, or use sexualized language or attention. Assume mistakes are correctable; repeated or severe misconduct is not.

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate these expectations. Report conduct concerns privately through repository maintainer contact or GitHub reporting features. Maintainers will protect reporter privacy where practical and apply proportionate action.
